# Augnes Product and Continuity Doctrine

> **Path continuity:** This doctrine retains the historical
> `01_AUGNES_VNEXT_MASTERPLAN.md` path so existing links remain stable.
>
> **Active ownership:** Product identity, continuity principles, cross-surface
> meaning, product responsibilities, complexity compression, and the product
> feature-change test.
>
> **Not owned here:** Core/protocol semantics, schemas, implementation status,
> sequencing, evaluation mechanics, or temporary C0–C9 program history.

## 1. Product definition

> **Augnes maintains the state, temporal lineage, evidence, perspectives,
> unresolved judgments, and next meaningful actions of long-horizon work, and
> projects the same product meaning into the host-native interfaces where the
> user is already working.**

> **The product invariant is the continuity of meaning, not the replication of
> one interface.**

> **The product is continuous work, not a collection of surfaces.**

Augnes is local-first and provider-neutral. Native hosts such as Browser,
ChatGPT Apps, Codex, and future hosts keep their own interaction grammar and
execution strengths. Augnes does not compete with them by rebuilding general
chat, code editing, terminal, browser, diff, pull-request, worktree, or
scheduler products.

Augnes owns continuity around that work: what the work is, how it changed over
time, what was observed, what supports a claim, what remains uncertain, what
requires human judgment, and what should matter next. A surface is useful only
when it helps the same durable work continue.

## 2. Product compass: Resume / Verify / Decide

### Resume

Recover the current coordinates of long-horizon work:

- work identity, scope, goal, and important constraints;
- the current meaningful situation and last meaningful change;
- accepted, rejected, stale, conflicted, or unresolved material;
- pending human judgment;
- the next meaningful action.

Resume quality is not the volume of stored context. It is the ability to reach
the first correct action with less repeated explanation and fewer wrong-context
corrections.

### Verify

Separate observation, attestation, evidence, claim, inference, uncertainty, and
result. Show what actually ran, what was checked, what was skipped, what is
source-anchored, and what remains unknown.

Execution completion is not verified success. A host result may be operational
residue without proving that the task goal was satisfied.

### Decide

Present the consequential judgment and its effect without making the user
operate the underlying protocol. Recommendation is not decision. Candidate is
not accepted state. A user decision is not an applied Transition.

If a capability does not materially improve Resume, Verify, or Decide, it is
unlikely to deserve default product presence.

## 3. Cross-surface continuity

Browser, ChatGPT Apps, Codex, and future hosts may differ in density, wording,
visual form, and available controls. They must not materially contradict one
another about:

- work identity and scope;
- current goal;
- important constraints;
- current meaningful situation;
- last meaningful change;
- verified observations and results;
- unresolved uncertainty, conflict, risk, or staleness;
- pending user judgment;
- next meaningful action;
- authority and execution boundary;
- relevant source anchors.

Cross-surface consistency does not require identical interfaces. Each host
should use its native strengths while preserving the same product meaning.

## 4. Continuous-work responsibilities

The responsibilities below describe one continuous product. They are not a
license to create one route, page, panel, card, status, or persistent record per
responsibility.

### Blank State: attention and resumption

Blank State is the default human-attention and resumption responsibility. It
centers long-lived work and consequential human intervention, not an alarm
dashboard, feature-card grid, or system taxonomy.

It should answer, in ordinary language:

- What am I trying to do?
- What is happening now?
- What changed since I last looked?
- Is anything blocked, stale, risky, or waiting for me?
- What is the one most meaningful next action?

An attention queue must be mature enough to avoid false human alarms. Engine
activity, a new record, or a merely possible action is not automatically worthy
of attention.

### AI Workplane: operational continuity

AI Workplane is the human projection plus operational layer for:

- delegation and bounded automation;
- meaningful progress and resumption;
- result ingestion and verification;
- evidence and claim reconciliation;
- uncertainty and conflict handling;
- review preparation and decision consequence;
- later-context preparation and outcome feedback;
- long-horizon research capabilities that remain inside authority boundaries.

AI Workplane coordinates native execution; it does not replicate a generic
agent shell or expose the internal research substrate by default.

### Work detail: temporal narrative

Work detail is timeline-first. It explains meaningful transitions in the work,
not an exhaustive event, commit, tool, model, protocol, or telemetry log.

The temporal narrative should make cause, change, current meaning, and the next
decision understandable. Exact exhaustive records remain available through
Inspector when needed.

### Relationship exploration: bounded connection view

Relationship exploration explains how relevant work, evidence, claims,
decisions, and outcomes connect. It is bounded by the user question and current
work. It is not a permanent “constellation” product, an exhaustive graph, or a
graph-management obligation.

### GuideBrief: present meaning

GuideBrief is an always-available, contextual, source-anchored,
conversational, and non-authoritative interpretation layer across product
surfaces and host-native interfaces.

It explains the current situation, meaningful change, verified basis, bounded
inference, uncertainty, risk, unresolved judgment, and next useful action. It
may recommend, but recommendation is not decision. It may explain authority,
but it cannot create or expand authority.

GuideBrief does not establish truth, accepted Perspective, durable state,
execution permission, user decision, or Transition. It does not replace the
exact task/run context contract defined by Core/protocol authority. The current
bounded implementation contract is
[`GUIDEBRIEF_CONTRACT_V0_2.md`](../GUIDEBRIEF_CONTRACT_V0_2.md); the fuller
conversational responsibility remains product direction until runtime evidence
shows it is implemented.

### How to use: stable product manual

“How to use” explains stable product concepts and supported operation. It is
not a live interpretation of current work and must not compete with GuideBrief.

### Inspector / Exact details: exact records

Inspector is optional, dense, neutral, read-only exact detail for provenance,
lineage, authority, diagnostics, and audit. A normal user must be able to
resume, follow progress, understand a result, and make the important decision
without opening Inspector or entering internal IDs.

The durable distinction is:

> **Timeline explains sequence.**
>
> **Relationship exploration explains connection.**
>
> **GuideBrief explains present meaning.**
>
> **Inspector exposes exact records.**

## 5. Long-horizon research substrate

Under AI Workplane, Augnes may develop:

- temporal continuity and long-term goal tracking;
- perspective formation, revision, counter-perspective, and falsification
  pressure;
- evidence, provenance, lineage, and causal relationship analysis;
- bounded strategic transfer;
- outcome-based learning and context-use feedback;
- metacognitive process improvement.

Research engines may explore, compare, challenge, infer, predict, and propose.
Research output is not truth, accepted Perspective, user decision, Transition,
execution authority, or authority expansion.

Formal structure, model agreement, graph density, a persistent artifact, or a
runtime implementation does not change this boundary. Insufficient support
remains uncertainty. Research value must eventually be demonstrated by later
outcomes, not by the number of engines, models, schemas, records, or views.

## 6. Complexity compression

> **Internal research complexity may increase; default user complexity must not
> increase with it.**

Implementation existence creates no UI entitlement:

- one object does not automatically become one card;
- one engine does not automatically become one page or menu;
- one protocol stage does not automatically become one visible workflow step;
- one status does not automatically become one badge;
- one relationship does not automatically become a permanent graph.

Default product projection shows the user goal, current meaningful situation,
important result or change, consequential uncertainty or risk, pending
judgment, and next meaningful action. Supporting information is progressively
disclosed; exact records remain available without becoming the normal path.

## 7. Product and Core authority boundary

This doctrine defines product meaning and responsibility. The sole authority
for Core records and protocol semantics is
[`02_AUGNES_VNEXT_ARCHITECTURE_AND_PROTOCOL.md`](./02_AUGNES_VNEXT_ARCHITECTURE_AND_PROTOCOL.md).

The following boundaries are durable:

- GuideBrief remains contextual, source-anchored, conversational, and
  non-authoritative.
- Research engines may explore, compare, challenge, infer, predict, and
  propose.
- Research output is not truth, accepted Perspective, user decision,
  Transition, execution authority, or authority expansion.
- Recommendation is not decision.
- Execution completion is not verified success.
- Candidate is not accepted state.
- Product projections do not create new Core records or authority.
- Semantic authority and execution authority are separate.
- Native permission is not Augnes approval.
- A user decision and an authorized Transition remain distinct.

Product projections may request or present a bounded Core operation. They do
not define a new protocol object or make a durable change merely by appearing
in a product doctrine or interface.

## 8. Local-first, provider-neutral continuity

The user-owned Augnes workspace is the continuity boundary for project state,
lineage, reviewed decisions, and selected reusable context. Provider memory,
model sessions, and host UI may assist but do not silently replace reviewed
project state.

External systems retain their own source of truth:

- repositories retain code and pull-request history;
- original sources retain their canonical content;
- agent hosts retain native execution;
- Augnes retains bounded references, receipts, evidence relations, reviewed
  decisions, continuity, and Perspective lineage.

Provider-specific IDs remain external references. Replacing a provider should
not require redefining product meaning or Core semantics.

## 9. Product feature-change test

Future product and research work must answer:

1. Which core user question does this improve?
2. What interpretation burden does the system absorb?
3. What durable product meaning must remain consistent across surfaces?
4. Why does this capability deserve any default user-facing presence?
5. Which existing surface absorbs it?
6. What existing surface, card, explanation, or concept is removed, demoted, or
   replaced?
7. How is uncertainty preserved?
8. How is user authority preserved?
9. How does the result return to long-term continuity?
10. What later outcome would demonstrate usefulness or reveal failure?

A capability that cannot answer these questions remains research, internal
capability, compatibility, or deferred work rather than default product UI.

## 10. Non-goals

Augnes is not:

- a new general-purpose AI work app or chatbot;
- a replacement for host-native execution, code, terminal, browser, diff,
  pull-request, worktree, or scheduling interfaces;
- an automatic truth, consensus, winner-selection, or Perspective-promotion
  system;
- an excuse to persist hidden reasoning, raw prompts, or broad transcripts;
- a graph database or “constellation” product by default;
- a license for automatic cross-project identity or Personal Perspective
  injection;
- a source of GitHub merge authority or unbounded external action.
