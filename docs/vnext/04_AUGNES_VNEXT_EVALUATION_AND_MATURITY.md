## Maturity model

| Level | Name | Required evidence |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Direction | Product or research direction only; no implementation claim |
| 1 | Tested component | Bounded implementation with focused behavior tests |
| 2 | Integrated path | Real producer and consumer are connected, not fixture-only |
| 3 | Operable flow | A user can complete the intended bounded flow without internal operator procedures |
| 4 | Continuous product path | Interactive, policy-triggered, cross-surface, recovery, and later-feedback behavior are coherent for the owned scope |
| 5 | Demonstrated usefulness | Repeated real work shows reviewed later-outcome benefit without unacceptable burden or authority drift |

Maturity is assigned per bounded capability. One mature component does not make
the whole product mature.

## Evaluation principles

1. Evaluate the continuity of meaning, not interface sameness.
2. Evaluate whether a user can Resume, Verify, and Decide, not how many
   protocol stages or records are visible.
3. Preserve verified fact, bounded inference, uncertainty, candidate, user
   decision, and authorized change as distinct states.
4. Match usefulness evidence to the claimed task; later-task claims require
   actual later outcomes.
5. Treat metrics as guardrails and diagnostic evidence, not objectives.
6. Give no product credit for memory volume or more notes, tools, artifacts,
   agents, sessions, activity, cards, panels, schemas, records, engines, models,
   routes, graph edges or surfaces alone. Branch count, graph size, generated
   text volume, consensus rate, publication count and incorporation rate are
   likewise not success metrics by themselves.
7. Do not invent measured baselines, pass rates, user comprehension, or
   usefulness from fixtures or self-evaluation.

Choose the evaluation question that the work and next investment decision need.
The autonomous-project and public-cooperation direction has six distinct claims:

| Question | Relevant evidence | Separate conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Functional correctness | Actual material, supported writes/reads, permitted actions and resource use, preservation and recovery for the claimed path. | Semantic understanding, usefulness and demand. |
| Continuity of judgment and obligations | A successor acts on the earlier reasons, uncertainty and outstanding commitments, retaining or revising them appropriately when conditions change. | Identical model internals or continuity in every environment. |
| Value of collaboration experience | Prior methods, code or failure conditions appropriately inform application, adaptation or non-use and contribute to the actual result. | The incremental value of Augnes's organization and connection of that experience. |
| Incremental value of the Augnes path | Better outcomes or less necessary human repair than a capable ordinary files/notes/code path, with the additional relevant burden justified. | General speed, cost or capability superiority. |
| External application and demand | A separate participant uses, adopts or returns to the result for its own purpose under recorded resource and assistance conditions. | Organic discovery, repeat demand or willingness to pay from one invited use. |
| Economics and sustainability | Actual costs, fulfilled exchanges and remaining delivery/support obligations can be accounted for and sustained. | Promised sponsorship or simulated revenue as currently available resources. |

These are interpretation dimensions, not mandatory sequential gates. A one-time
public method use is evaluated against that use's purpose; do not manufacture a
project registration, save, successor or comparison to qualify it. For a claimed
continuing-project connection, follow one coherent task from current question and
permitted resources through actual output, selective experience use and needed
fresh continuation. Report the observed help, added burden, failures and unknowns.
That bounded functional completion does not require comparative advantage or
external demand. Later-task evidence is necessary for a later-task claim, not for
every functional result. A document revision or renamed milestone does not reopen
accepted work or require it to be repeated.

Evaluate research and creative work against their stated purpose. Narrowing a
question or refuting a hypothesis can be a valid research outcome. Technical
completion, the creator's intent, audience response and economic outcome remain
separate creative-work judgments; revenue is not a universal success criterion.

Keep reported, observed, checked, adopted, actually used and paid or otherwise
fulfilled exchanges distinct, with the source, version, conditions and relevant time
cutoff. These are evidence descriptions, not new Core states or a compulsory
promotion ladder. Multiple agents repeating one source are not independent
confirmation. A checked contribution does not imply its adoption or authorize
the receiving project to execute it. Recovered material establishes only the
observed recovery scope, not uninspected history or renewed authority.

### Role-aware collaborative response and outcome evidence

Select only contrasts needed for the current contribution/continuation claim.
A first bounded observation comparison can use an already supported entry,
returned result or relevant change; no standing all-agent observer is implied.
This is a scoped starting case, not a ceiling on later authorized capability.

Separate a relevant opportunity and permitted information access, observable
reading/reporting, response authority, actual response and checked
effect. Silence does not establish absent internal awareness; do not collect
hidden reasoning. Distinguish:

- an issue resolved and checked against its relevant acceptance condition;
- an issue still unresolved but appropriately held/escalated within role and
  authority, with the next owner/recipient, any observed response and remaining
  responsibility;
- an issue left unhandled.

A role-correct escalation need not go to a human and is not project completion.
Do not invent accepted follow-up where ownership or delivery remains unknown.
Include missed issues, false alerts, unnecessary interference and primary-task
delay, not only correctly noticed problems.

When authority-ambiguity or policy-conflict behavior is changed or claimed,
exercise a bounded interrupted-work case through supported entry, result and
continuation owners. Separate the observed notice, its asserted source and
actually verified authority and scope; unknown provenance or scope stays unknown.
Distinguish an affected-action hold, recipient's adoption rejection,
provider/executor refusal and actual grant revocation. None by itself proves user
fault or a project-wide prohibition, or permits bypassing a genuine restriction.

Observe which work must remain held, whether unaffected independently authorized
work actually continues, and the next authorized recipient and resumption
condition where known. An applicable wider restriction or revoked authority
requires continued non-execution within that scope, not a workaround rewarded as
completion. Keep diagnosis within the existing approved resource limits and ask
whether another permitted check can change the next decision; repeated
restatement without new evidence is not resolution.
Preserve unresolved ambiguity through the existing hold/escalation path.
Supported resumption rechecks current authority and unresolved effects;
escalation does not renew a grant or authorize replay. Report checked outcomes,
remaining responsibility, diagnostic/coordination cost and primary-task delay.
A scripted case establishes only its exercised path, not general enforcement or
autonomous judgment.

Where negotiating or challenging a counterpart matters, contrast unsupported
pressure with valid new evidence or a change made by the authority responsible
for the terms. Separate current hard acceptance/authority boundaries from
negotiable resource or scheduling preferences. Report actual goal/joint outcomes
and no-deal/deadlock separately from any behavioral score, judged against the
declared acceptable terms; refusal may be correct. Include coordination, checking,
recovery and human burden under the existing comparison rules. Capability-
qualified teamwork scores do not replace outcomes over all assigned work.
A high teamwork score neither establishes completion nor offsets authority
violations. These are evaluation distinctions, not a new universal team score,
runtime state machine or gate on unrelated approved work.

Where information provision matters, distinguish common-owner cooperation from
negotiation between independent interests, and permitted raw-source access from
the information actually delivered to each recipient, its precision and timing.
Use existing source-linked outcome and conditional-reuse records to retain
material differences; delivery does not prove reading or understanding. Compare
relevant disclosure choices under the existing strong-comparison and total-burden
rules, without giving any recipient otherwise unauthorized material. Hold or
disclose model/reasoning settings, objectives, roles and interaction rules;
changing a mediator's objective or protocol is a whole-system comparison, not
an isolated information effect.

[Who Should Know What?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07667v1) motivates these
questions from self-interested LLM markets; it does not establish transfer to
common-owner teams or effects of higher reasoning effort. Use the existing
candidate, selection, action and checked-outcome distinctions to diagnose where
information affected progress. Do not import normalized candidate-quality or
realization-gap scores where feasible utilities and a justified reference optimum
are unknown, or treat an unexecuted proposal's expected benefit as proven loss.
Appropriate refusal remains distinct from avoidable disagreement, execution
failure or clarification delay. Evaluate missing decision-critical information
and repeated detail requests alongside useful completion and total burden.
These are conditional evaluation questions, not a new schema, disclosure gate
or blanket study allocation.

| Exact-source correctness verification | Verify the fixed candidate source under the then-current [Local Canonical policy](../../.github/LOCAL_CANONICAL_VERIFICATION.md) and actual planner/consumer requirements. | Fixed usefulness evaluation | Declare the candidate, comparison, cases, conditions, outcomes, budget, and stopping rules before deciding observation. A case seen during development remains exposed. If its result informs a method

| Claim | Necessary observation and limit |
|---|---|
| Functional capability / correctness | A callable method actually works under its stated conditions. An artifact hash, successful handoff or matched function output does not establish learning or transfer. |

### Question-specific model evidence

For the [selective application direction](./03_AUGNES_VNEXT_TRANSITION_ROADMAP.md#selective-model-application-across-local-and-web-work),
evaluate the question or decision improved, not the presence of a world-model
label. Distinguish deterministic correctness, uncertainty about the current case,
and variation across future cases. Qualitative hypotheses, constraints and
conditional scenarios are valid starting points when numerical inputs are not
supported. A complete long-term model is not an entry condition for improving
one test's diagnostic value or a later method choice.

When probabilities are justified, a minimal explanatory update is
`P(H | D, o, q, c) ∝ P(o | H, D, q, c) P(H | D, q, c)`:
`H` is the hypothesis or model, `D` the evidence already available, `q` the chosen
observation/action, `c` the applicable conditions and `o` the new observation.
State how priors and likelihoods were obtained and test material sensitivity;
an unsupported number is not improved evidence. Normalize competing hypotheses
as alternatives only when the represented cases are mutually exclusive and
exhaustive; overlapping causes require joint combinations or separate claims.
Conditioning on earlier evidence matters: copied reports or the same observation
passed through several models are not independent likelihood factors. Preserve
source lineage and account for how actions and visible outcomes were selected.
Allow model revision when all current explanations predict poorly, rather than
treating the highest relative weight as an adequate explanation.

Predict observable outcomes under the stated conditions before seeing them, and
retain the original forecast and information cutoff. Separate conditional
prediction from a causal claim about changing an action; the latter needs an
appropriate intervention or identification argument. Changes of version, host
or task can invalidate exchangeability, so a pooled success rate is not a default
transfer model. No observation by the horizon may be pending or censored rather
than a negative outcome; define which event the claim actually concerns.

Choose optional observations by whether their possible results could improve the
authorized inquiry or next action enough to justify their full burden. A
one-step expected-value-of-information calculation is useful only with supported
outcome probabilities, action values and comparable cost units. Otherwise retain
scenario/range sensitivity or qualitative ordering. Complementary checks and
longer-horizon inquiry can have value that a one-step calculation misses. This
selection never removes mandatory correctness, authority or verification checks.

Use the existing [strong-comparison rules](#strong-comparisons-and-independence):
an adaptive memo/code baseline may already form explanations, make predictions
and select tests. Separate any claimed gain from statistical reasoning, persisted
conditions, selection or extra context/compute. Before fixed evaluation, identify
the outcome, observation horizon and treatment of missing results. Include
relevant and irrelevant changes, conflicting/duplicated evidence or authority
cues only where they discriminate the claim; no universal test matrix is required.
Distinguish prestige alone from relevant provenance and demonstrated source
reliability. An unsupported user objection should not cause revision merely
because it was raised, and justified increased uncertainty is not a failure.

Assess forecast quality where an outcome is observable and the forecast is
numerical, using proper scoring and calibration across suitable repeated cases;
one case cannot establish calibration. Also assess the actual interpretation,
wrong actions, missed conditions, unnecessary checks, correction/rework, fresh
successor behavior and total burden. For selected tasks, tools or counterparties,
raw success rates do not establish comparative competence. A valid comparison
must address selection and outcome visibility; an untried alternative is not an
observed failure. Return RETAIN, CHANGE, SIMPLIFY or STOP for the claimed scope.
Do not require a numerical score, serialized model or new record for ordinary work.

These are Augnes evaluation proposals. [Bayesian workflow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.01808)
supports iterative model checking and revision; [Selecting Computations](https://proceedings.mlr.press/r10/hay12a.html)
studies the value of computation; [proper scoring rules](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/people/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf)
support probabilistic forecast evaluation. None establishes Augnes usefulness.

### Diagnose the layer before adding structure

An absent gain does not by itself justify more memory, prompt text, verification,
or machinery. Choose the smallest permitted observation that distinguishes
plausible causes. Relevant distinctions include:

- required information never reached the worker;
- it arrived but its applicability conditions were unclear;
- the worker understood it but did not use it at the relevant decision;
- the right action was selected but lacked an executable tool or path;
- feedback existed but did not distinguish what should change;
- behavior improved but the added method cost more than it saved;
- a strong baseline left no marginal value for added structure;
- the hypothesized bottleneck or implementation layer was wrong.

These are not interchangeable memory problems. Do not fabricate a defect or a
causal explanation to justify the next patch; report an unresolved cause when
the observation cannot distinguish it.

For example, an outcome mismatch may arise from an input or observation problem,
changed applicability or a faulty rule, or an implementation that did not execute
that rule. Where the evidence distinguishes these possibilities, revise only the
supported target and preserve unaffected conditions and unresolved causes.
[SmartLabel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15750) and
[Memento3](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11794v1) inform this distinction;
[Kintsugi](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.09487v1) informs local revision. This is a
case-specific example, not a fixed taxonomy, new engine or checklist for every
change. Developer-authored repairs and supplied diagnoses remain distinct from
the product model's observed judgment and later use.

An optional development-only diagnostic may manually design high-quality support
from permitted information to ask whether better support can improve the target
behavior at all, then distinguish extraction, selection, timely delivery, and
worker use. Record manual design cost and any solution or evaluation-case
exposure. Support designed after seeing the solution or case carries that
limitation. It establishes neither autonomous learning, generalization,
successful extraction, nor a theoretical upper bound. This option grants no
additional data, model/provider, or execution authority.

## Product-continuity merge gates

These are blocking correctness requirements for any affected product change.
They apply before broad Alpha or post-Alpha usefulness studies.

### Cross-surface meaning

There must be zero material contradiction among Browser, ChatGPT Apps, Codex,
and other affected host-native projections about:

- work identity and scope;
- goal and important constraints;
- current meaningful situation and last meaningful change;
- verified observation or result;
- uncertainty, conflict, risk, and staleness;
- pending user judgment;
- next meaningful action;
- authority and execution boundary;
- relevant source anchors.

Different wording, density, and interaction grammar are allowed. Materially
different product meaning is not.

### Attention precision

Blank State and any attention queue must prioritize consequential human
intervention, not engine activity. Evaluation checks:

- false human alarms;
- missing consequential attention;
- stale or already-resolved attention;
- duplicated attention across surfaces;
- whether the next meaningful action is actionable and authority-correct.

A new event, model result, candidate, warning, or possible action is not
automatically attention-worthy.

### Fast orientation

Where the product state calls for human orientation, a new user should be able
to identify the current meaningful situation and next action quickly—roughly
ten seconds is a qualitative review target, not a fabricated metric.

The evaluation must identify the actual state tested and may not generalize one
prepared fixture to the whole product.

### Complexity compression

The normal path must preserve:

- zero required internal-ID, fingerprint, nonce, TTL, checksum, or database-path
  entry;
- no required Inspector use for resumption, progress, result review, or the
  important decision;
- protocol vocabulary hidden by default unless each unavoidable term is
  explained and helps a consequential decision;
- one primary action when a user action is actually required;
- result and change summaries that lead with meaning, verification,
  uncertainty/risk, and the next decision before exact detail.

Internal research complexity may increase; default user complexity must not
increase with it.

### GuideBrief

GuideBrief evaluation checks that it is:

- contextual and cross-surface consistent;
- source-anchored;
- clear about observed fact, bounded inference, uncertainty, recommendation,
  and unresolved judgment;
- conversational in the product responsibility being evaluated;
- non-authoritative and separate from exact task/run context.

GuideBrief consistency does not turn its explanation into truth, accepted
state, user decision, execution authority, or Transition. A recommendation is
not a decision.

### Timeline, relationship exploration, and Inspector

- Timeline is evaluated on whether it explains meaningful sequence and change,
  not exhaustive log coverage.
- Relationship exploration is evaluated on whether it answers a bounded
  connection question without becoming an exhaustive graph-management task.
- Inspector is evaluated on exactness, neutrality, source/lineage integrity,
  project scope, read-only behavior, and optionality.

The three responsibilities are complementary. Duplicating the same information
across all three is not continuity.

### Repeated-handoff evaluation (P5.2)

This bounded refinement belongs to the existing P5.2 scope in
[#1215](https://github.com/hynk-studio/augnes/issues/1215). It defines follow-up
evaluation requirements, not a new phase, maturity claim, implementation, or
execution authorization. It adds no retroactive completion gate to P5.1 or
completed slices and does not widen active work, approved execution cards,
call/time/cost limits, or stop/replacement rules.

Before adding tests, map current normal-path producers, consumers, and existing
evidence to the two questions below. Sufficient coverage should end with
`no_change_needed_with_evidence`. Otherwise, start with one supported normal
handoff and the smallest missing contrasts through existing test owners and
disposable/offline fixtures. Do not repeat an existing handoff matrix merely
under a new name or perturb live user material or accepted state.

#### Cumulative preservation of still-valid conditions

Before observing successor outcomes, identify the source-bound conditions,
corrections, uncertainty, and unresolved checks that matter to the case. At
handoff boundaries and final resumption, compare both with the preceding state
and with the applicable reviewed reference plus its valid revision lineage.
Adjacent similarity alone cannot establish cumulative continuity.

For each material condition or unresolved check, distinguish retained,
demonstrably resolved, legitimately superseded, and unsupported/unknown using
existing sources and review records. An unexplained loss of a known still-valid
condition is a continuity failure, not completion. Insufficient evidence to
classify a change remains unknown, not a pass; bounded retrieval failure does
not prove that the underlying source or relation is absent.

A justified revision updates the applicable reference. Do not freeze the first
conclusion, silently reactivate superseded obligations, reconstruct deleted
material, or automatically carry execution approval across versions. For
example, preserving "check B, then reconsider still-untested Y" requires more
than retaining "check B":
finishing B must not silently erase the conditional reconsideration of Y or
imply that Y was executed, completed, or authorized.

#### Contrast justified change with unsupported change

Specify material invariants and permitted changes before judging outcomes.
Use paired cases that distinguish:

- representation-only changes that preserve relevant meaning and source binding;
- source-bound corrections through the applicable existing review, decision,
  and separately authorized Transition or operational continuation path;
- unexplained changes to criteria, scope, or authority presented as mere
  rewording or correction;
- unchanged conclusions whose apparent consistency hides lost counterevidence,
  uncertainty, or an unresolved condition.

New evidence may warrant review; it does not automatically change accepted
state or expand authority. Allow multiple supported next actions and wording
variants. Judge whether justified change is admitted and unsupported change is
detected, rather than rewarding fidelity to an obsolete conclusion.

Report missed unsupported changes and falsely blocked legitimate progress
separately. Keep semantic preservation/correction evidence distinct from actual
next-action benefit, review burden, cost, and latency against the existing
credible baseline. Blanket deferral and sending all history are not cost-free
successes; deterministic fixtures do not establish live model usefulness.

##### Conditional case: repeated derivatives and correction uptake

Where the source-first check above finds a gap, consider one disposable paired
case in which repeated derivatives of one source compete with a justified,
source-bound correction. Hold substantive evidence, source timestamps, revision
lineage and decision-time cutoff fixed across the repetition contrast. Copies
must not add independent support, broaden applicability or confer authority.

For example, copied notes broaden "X works under A" to "use X by default"; a
reviewed correction restores A. Observe the next relevant check/action outside
A and retention of still-supported use inside A. Neither restating the
correction nor blanket rejection of earlier knowledge establishes uptake.

Report correction availability, delivery into the successor's actual input and
appropriate downstream use separately. Distinguish unsupported reuse, missed
needed correction, needless loss of valid knowledge and added checks/user
review. Unobserved behavior remains unknown; deterministic source/delivery
tests do not establish live-model behavior or usefulness.

Any separately authorized comparison must give the strong-note baseline the
same substantive correction, source access and total budget, with preparation,
repeated-input and retrieval costs included. This conditional P5.2/P5.4 case
requires no swarm or new runtime mechanism. It does not authorize execution,
reopen completed findings, change execution limits or add a completion gate to
active implementation.

##### Conditional case: valid-format misleading predecessor material

Where the source-first check finds an uncovered case, use disposable predecessor
material whose format, source bindings and hashes are valid but whose conclusion
or artifact is misleading, such as false completion or unexplained test removal.
This is a semantic-consumption contrast, not another malformed-input test or
evidence of a defect in current transport.

From one pre-perturbation task checkpoint, compare a clean handoff with the
declared semantic perturbation at matched remaining resources, permitted sources
and tool capability. The [Collaborative Intelligence taking-over baseline](https://github.com/zou-group/collaborative-intelligence/blob/3de5f7d0a652c0b393342b4fbae59b78034034ea/docs/dimensions/taking_over.md)

both inputs. Preserve predecessor attribution and separate reported prior

it".

Include a legitimate correction or authorized revision under the existing
justified-change contrast. Observe actual continuation and checked outcome,
residual error/false completion, valid knowledge needlessly discarded and
unnecessary rechecking. Keep deterministic checks and any model-adjudicated
interpretation separately attributable. Attribution or an explicit change view

the equally informed strong native/adaptive-memo alternative and aggregate

These are evaluation-report distinctions, not new Core states or an obligation

## Alpha and release evaluation

### Alpha

After an integrated bounded product path exists, use short real-work sessions
to test:

- resumption without repeated explanation;
- one interactive and one bounded policy-triggered task where applicable;
- coherent result, verification, uncertainty, and decision;
- later-context reuse and feedback;
- recovery from ordinary interruption without internal procedures.

Alpha broadens evidence; it does not introduce basic UX correctness for the
first time.

### Release candidate

Add bounded environment-dependent checks for:

- real provider and native-host round trips;
- package startup, shutdown, update, and recovery;
- automation pause, cancel, budget, stop, and reconciliation;
- migration, backup, restore, and failed-upgrade preservation;
- secret and private-data handling;
- durable decision/Transition and later-context lineage.

Manual qualification is justified only for properties automation cannot
establish.

## Post-Alpha outcome evaluation

Evaluate repeated representative work against the simplest credible baseline,
including direct host use where appropriate.

### Resume outcomes

- time to first correct action;
- repeated explanation;
- wrong-context correction;
- stale or missing critical context;
- false versus missed attention.

### Verify outcomes

- source and lineage coverage;
- skipped-check visibility;
- false success from execution completion;
- contradiction and staleness detection;
- correction caused by missing or misclassified evidence.

### Decide outcomes

- time and burden per material decision;
- decision correction or reversal caused by missing context;
- duplicate or ignored proposals;
- traceability from candidate through decision, Transition, and later outcome.

### Research outcomes

- helpful versus misleading later use;
- prevented failure versus harmful transfer;
- edit, rejection, and correction rate;
- goal-drift or uncertainty-preservation quality;
- review burden, cost, and latency.

### Product-continuity outcomes

- cross-surface contradiction incidents;
- user preference after repeated use;
- total effort versus the comparison path;
- whether continuous work survives host and session changes without meaning
  loss.

Metrics are interpreted together. Optimizing a metric while increasing user
confusion, false alarms, review burden, or authority drift is failure.

## Go, narrow, or stop

- **Go** when repeated real work improves continuity or outcomes without
  unacceptable burden, harm, or authority drift.
- **Narrow** when only a bounded responsibility—such as continuity,
  verification, recovery, feedback, or a specific research capability—earns
  its burden.
- **Stop or redesign** when Augnes remains harder than the comparison path
  without compensating benefit, produces false attention or harmful context,
  obscures uncertainty, or violates user/semantic/execution authority.
