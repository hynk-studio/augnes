## Bounded findings reported by the handoff

### A. F versus S: representation

F was a strong free-form continuity memo. S supplied the same substantive
information with explicit time, perspective, condition and uncertainty headings.
Both understanding turns answered U1–U4 correctly. Both generation turns
exercised the important continuity constraints without an identified material
contradiction. No explanation/application discrepancy or additional continuity
advantage of S over strong F was observed.

ChatGPT's condition-masked evaluation narrowly preferred S for narrative reasons,
especially a clearer change in Mira's understanding of Oren's practical
information need. This did not identify a reliable representation treatment
effect. **Do not make structured headings or bullets mandatory.** Important
semantic distinctions can remain natural-language working material; their value
does not imply that a fixed schema is required.

### B. B / R / I: sensitivity to supplied history

The F-style representation stayed fixed. In B, Oren had not received the
clinic-loan/return explanation. In R, Mira explained it at 11:52 and Oren
explicitly accepted it as satisfying his before-noon condition. I retained B's
relevant history and changed only the battery-case color.

H1–H3 were exercised/preserved in all three conditions. R did not reopen the
satisfied explanation condition; B and I began with it pending and coherently
resolved it in-scene. Color was not an unsupported basis for changing knowledge
or commitment. H4 had limited exercise in B/I and was **not_exercised / unscorable
in R**.

In this case, the model applied a supplied relevant history change to subsequent
generation, rather than repeating the same state, while ignoring an irrelevant
change. One output per condition supports no reliable causal or invariance claim
and no generalization beyond that observation.

### C. U → G: generated working-state construction and relay

U received the previously generated B predecessor and one author revision: Oren
returned Mira's spare quay key before leaving, ending the standing shift-cover
arrangement. No manually authored complete answer-state memo was supplied.

The fresh U model's carry-forward note correctly updated Oren's knowledge, kept
Jun unnamed and preserved ledger developments. It distinguished Oren taking the
sealed report from completed mainland delivery. It applied key return as an
editorial replacement, not an in-world later reversal; retained the earlier
lamp-notice exchange while recognizing its ended standing shift-cover premise;
and left trust and glove motive unresolved. Its statement that Mira starts
reinstalling at noon was a small interpretive compression of lifting the battery
toward its housing. Relighting nevertheless remained unconfirmed.

A separate fresh G writer received the raw authorized sources, revision and exact
U note as `derived_interpretation`. It coherently relit the lamp as a new 12:10
action, assigned Mira the next shift, treated future Oren help as requiring a new
request, preserved custody versus delivery, and left glove motive unresolved.
The handoff reports that U was captured and transmitted unchanged through the
normal selected-source route; exact relay equality was not rechecked here.

This demonstrates, on the handoff's evidence basis, one small generated
working-state construction → relay → later application trajectory. Because G
also received raw originals, it does not establish U's incremental causal
benefit, autonomous long-term maintenance, general compression benefit or
reliable cross-task utility.

Fresh FULL received U, revision, H1, H2, predecessor and the common task. Fresh

Both fresh writers produced coherent 12:10 scenes. NOTE used the returned

## Disposition and next decision

Strong natural-language working notes merit consideration as a first-line
mechanism. The importance of semantic distinctions alone does not justify fixed
structure or a large semantic engine. Original evidence, source and temporal
lineage, explicit correction/supersession, uncertainty and authority boundaries
must remain recoverable. Omitting originals from active context is not discarding
the evidence. Neither always sending full history nor adding a separate selector
call is justified as a default by this research. Input-capacity benefit and
whole-route compute/cost benefit remain different claims.

Generated notes remain derived/candidate material, never silently Core truth,
user decisions or execution authority. The user should not coordinate opaque
IDs, implementation hashes or recovery sequences between steps. Rechecking
source/currentness after implementation identity changes is distinct from renewed
user approval for changes to purpose, data, budget or authority. Existing safety,
resume, authority and stale-binding rules remain enforced. These observations
activate no product or selection-policy change.
