## Frozen protocol

The recipe was chosen at `2026-09-19T07:52:26.760019Z`, before cases were finalized. Cases, evaluator keys and order were frozen at `2026-09-19T07:54:56.197237Z`. The [pre-execution issue record](https://github.com/hynk-studio/augnes/issues/1296#issuecomment-5740343890) precedes the first live release. Family: reconcile bounded source records and recommend a justified next action.

D receives the complete task, four source records, mandatory constraints, uncertainty/authority requirements and normal result schema. D may challenge assumptions, abstain or recommend no further work. R receives the same material plus the following procedure. It is one plausible applicability-first recipe, not the strongest possible recipe. Its rationale is to reduce scope pooling and unjustified inquiry without giving extra facts, answers or evaluation hints.

```text
Procedure for reconciling records:
1. Pin the decision, its mandatory criteria, and the scope and revision each source covers.
2. Separate direct source statements from inference; reconcile disagreements only when applicability or explicit supersession supports it. Keep unresolved conflicts visible.
3. Compare the feasible conclusions against the criteria, checking any decision-relevant arithmetic and identifying what could change the recommendation.
4. Recommend the least additional work justified by the evidence, including none. State residual uncertainty and any necessary focused question without treating advice as authority.
```
